# Introduction

In 2025, I designed and fabricated a custom **FPV racing drone** achieving a peak speed of **240 km/h** with a total weight of just 610g. The frame was custom-designed in Blender and 3D printed in **PET-CF** (carbon fiber reinforced) for an optimal strength-to-weight ratio.

I sourced all electronics from Shenzhen, including a Mamba flight controller, high-discharge LiPo batteries, and a DJI O4 Pro air unit for HD video transmission.

# Design & Fabrication

The frame was designed entirely in **Blender**, exported for 3D printing in PET-CF (carbon fiber reinforced PET) which provides excellent rigidity while keeping weight minimal.

**Key Specs:**
- **Weight:** 610g all-up
- **Top Speed:** 240 km/h
- **Frame:** Custom Blender design, 3D printed in PET-CF
- **FC:** Mamba flight controller
- **VTX:** DJI O4 Pro air unit
- **Batteries:** High-discharge LiPo
- **Firmware:** Betaflight with custom PID tuning
